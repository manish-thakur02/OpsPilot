# DEMO-POL-01 — Incident prioritization
Synthetic demonstration policy. Version 1.0, 2026-10-23. Source: OpsPilot illustrative knowledge base. Owner: fictional demonstration service desk. This is not an official Accenture policy, an organizational policy, or a UCI source document.

## Assess impact and urgency
Record affected services, the scope of disruption, available workarounds and time sensitivity. The incident coordinator assigns a provisional priority and records the rationale. Reassess priority when impact or urgency changes; preserve the original assessment in the observation history. Missing information requires investigation, not invented details.

## Illustrative priority mapping
For this demonstration, P1 corresponds to the dataset label `1 - Critical`, P2 to `2 - High`, P3 to `3 - Moderate` and P4 to `4 - Low`. This is an illustrative mapping, not proof that this policy governed the historical UCI incidents. P1 means a critical service outage with widespread impact and no acceptable workaround. P2 means serious degradation with limited workarounds. P3 covers a localized disruption with manageable impact. P4 covers minor issues or low-urgency service questions.

## Approval and audit
The coordinator reviews increases to P1 immediately with the incident manager. Record the approving role, reason, time and customer impact before reducing priority. Do not erase prior classifications. Dataset priority_changed indicates observed changes, not whether those changes followed this procedure.
