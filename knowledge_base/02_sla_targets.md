# DEMO-POL-02 — Illustrative service targets
Synthetic demonstration policy. Version 1.0, 2026-10-23. Source: OpsPilot illustrative knowledge base. Owner: fictional demonstration service desk. Not an official Accenture policy, organizational contract, or UCI source document.

## Demonstration targets
Illustrative targets are: P1 (`1 - Critical`) acknowledgement within 15 minutes and restoration within 4 elapsed hours; P2 (`2 - High`) acknowledgement within 30 minutes and restoration within 8 elapsed hours; P3 (`3 - Moderate`) acknowledgement within 4 hours and restoration within 48 elapsed hours; P4 (`4 - Low`) acknowledgement within 8 hours and restoration within 120 elapsed hours. These examples use elapsed time, not business calendars. They are training assumptions rather than contractual obligations.

## Reporting boundaries
Restoration and resolution are different milestones. A target assessment would require verified service scope, agreed calendars, clock start/pause rules and actual restoration timestamps. The UCI source made_sla Boolean has not been verified to represent breach or compliance. Show its raw distribution or observed changes only. Never combine these illustrative targets with historical resolution durations and present the result as genuine SLA compliance.

## Approaching a target
Notify the coordinator when progress suggests the illustrative target is at risk. Escalate dependencies, record the reason and issue a revised estimate. Do not alter timestamps or mark an unresolved service as restored to meet a target.
