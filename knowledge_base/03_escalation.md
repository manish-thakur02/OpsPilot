# DEMO-POL-03 — Escalation procedure
Synthetic demonstration policy. Version 1.0, 2026-10-23. Source: OpsPilot illustrative knowledge base. Owner: fictional demonstration incident manager. Not an official Accenture policy, actual employer policy, or UCI source document.

## Critical P1 escalation
For a critical or highest-priority incident, use illustrative P1, mapped to dataset category `1 - Critical`. Immediately notify the incident manager and service owner. Establish a coordination bridge, appoint a technical lead and communications owner, and gather impact, affected services, start time and current workaround evidence. Engage the relevant resolver team immediately. The manager decides whether to declare a major incident; priority alone does not establish a historical major-incident classification. Provide stakeholder updates every 30 minutes until service restoration, including what is known, what is uncertain and the next action.

## High P2 escalation
For illustrative P2 (`2 - High`), notify the resolver lead and service owner. Escalate to the incident manager if impact grows, a critical dependency blocks progress or the illustrative service target is at risk. Document the owner and next checkpoint. The acknowledgement target in DEMO-POL-02 is 30 minutes; it is not a claim about historical dataset compliance.

## Functional and hierarchical handoff
Use functional escalation to obtain technical expertise. Use hierarchical escalation to obtain authority, resources or business decisions. Keep one accountable coordinator throughout. Record the handoff reason, receiving owner and confirmation; transfer evidence with the incident rather than repeatedly asking the reporter for the same information.
