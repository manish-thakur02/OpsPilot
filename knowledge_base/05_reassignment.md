# DEMO-POL-05 — Reassignment guidelines
Synthetic demonstration policy. Version 1.0, 2026-10-23. Source: OpsPilot illustrative knowledge base. Owner: fictional demonstration resolver lead. Not an official Accenture policy or a UCI source document.

## Evidence-based ownership transfer
Before reassignment, document why the receiving team is better placed to act. Include symptoms, tests, findings, impacted service and pending actions. Confirm the receiving team's ownership. The current coordinator remains accountable until the handoff is acknowledged. Escalate unclear ownership to the service owner rather than circulating a ticket across teams.

## Repeated transfers
When an incident has been reassigned twice without progress, the coordinator should review the route with the resolver lead. For P1 incidents, use direct collaboration on the incident bridge instead of sequential transfers. Preserve the transfer history and avoid resetting clocks merely because ownership changed.

## Using the historical fields
The UCI reassignment_count is an observed source counter, not a full narrative of each handoff. A high value is an investigation signal, not proof of poor performance. Assignment-group labels are anonymized and missing values remain unknown. OpsPilot's top-groups chart counts unique incidents by their latest observed group; it does not count every team that ever touched an incident.
