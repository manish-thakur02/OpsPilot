# DEMO-POL-06 — Major incident coordination
Synthetic demonstration policy. Version 1.0, 2026-10-23. Source: OpsPilot illustrative knowledge base. Owner: fictional demonstration incident manager. Not an official Accenture policy or a UCI source document.

## Declare and coordinate
The incident manager declares a major incident when disruption is widespread or poses critical business impact. Appoint an incident commander, technical lead, communications owner and timeline recorder. Establish one coordination bridge and track hypotheses, evidence, actions and decisions. A P1 classification should trigger immediate review under DEMO-POL-03, but not every P1 is automatically a declared major incident.

## Restore and communicate
Prioritize safe restoration and reversible mitigation. Record change decisions and risks. Send stakeholder updates every 30 minutes while a major outage continues, including confirmed impact, current action and next update time. Avoid unsupported root-cause claims. Validate restoration through service checks and affected-user confirmation where possible.

## Review and learning
Hold an illustrative post-incident review within five working days of restoration. Separate timeline facts from hypotheses, identify contributing conditions and assign follow-up owners. Do not blame anonymized individuals or infer major-incident counts from the UCI priority labels. The historical dataset does not supply verified declaration records, full narratives or customer communications.
