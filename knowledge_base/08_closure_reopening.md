# DEMO-POL-08 — Closure and reopening
Synthetic demonstration policy. Version 1.0, 2026-10-23. Source: OpsPilot illustrative knowledge base. Owner: fictional demonstration service desk. Not an official Accenture policy or a UCI source document.

## Closure evidence
Before closure, document the resolution, verification checks, requester communication and outstanding follow-up work. Confirm that service has been restored and record an accurate closure reason. If validation fails, return to investigation. Create a separate problem record for long-term root-cause work rather than claiming the incident remains unresolved solely because preventive work continues.

## Reopening procedure
Reopen an incident when the original symptom recurs or the reported resolution fails verification. Record the reason, new evidence and impact, reassess priority, notify the accountable resolver group and increment the reopening counter. Preserve the previous resolution and closure information as historical evidence. A separate unrelated interruption should receive a new incident linked where appropriate. Escalate a reopened critical incident using DEMO-POL-03.

## Historical interpretation
OpsPilot counts incidents with any recorded reopen_count greater than zero. This is an observed counter measure, not a verified reconstruction of every reopen transition. Missing narratives prevent confirmation that historical reopenings followed this synthetic procedure. Latest summary durations describe the latest observed record only and are not evidence of SLA compliance or complete customer experience.
